AirPlay for Q2 Pod: what the Q2 plays, on an AirPlay speaker (Sonos, HomePod, Apple TV, AirPlay 1
speakers), chosen from Now Playing's AirPlay button. Needs a Q2 Pod build with AirPlay. See README.md.

Install: **System settings → Apps → AirPlay**, or unzip to the card's root with the Q2 off.
